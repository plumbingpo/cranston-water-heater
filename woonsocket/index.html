Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Woonsocket

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush every year in hard-water areas.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually includes the manufacture date, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank heaters reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and is also showing discolored water, rumbling, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install after a leak.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a flooded utility room.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Woonsocket

Whatever type of water heater your Woonsocket home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
